## Best places in Sharm El Sheikh and Hurghada

Sharm El Sheikh and Hurghada are Egyptian holiday destinations known for beaches, turquoise water and marine activities. Whether you want to relax by the sea or explore on a boat or underwater, each city offers a different experience.

### Places to visit in Sharm El Sheikh

**Naama Bay.** Naama Bay is a popular visitor area with beaches, restaurants, cafés and entertainment. Its promenade is also an option for an evening walk.

**Ras Mohammed Protectorate.** This protected area is known for coral reefs, colourful fish and clear water. Visitors can explore its marine environment through diving or snorkelling where permitted.

**Tiran Island.** The waters around Tiran are known for coral reefs and marine life and are a destination for boat trips, diving and snorkelling. Check current access and operator arrangements before planning a visit.

**The Old Market.** For a local atmosphere or souvenirs, explore the shops, restaurants and cafés in Sharm El Sheikh's Old Market.

### Places to visit in Hurghada

**Giftun Island.** Giftun is known for sandy beaches and clear turquoise water. Boat excursions can offer opportunities for swimming and snorkelling; check the itinerary and protected-area rules with the operator.

**Hurghada Marina.** The marina offers a setting for a walk or a break at a restaurant or café overlooking the sea and boats.

**Al-Arqiya marine area.** Marine areas near Hurghada have varied coral reefs and sea life that may appeal to divers and snorkellers. The name and the exact location in the Arabic source need editorial verification before publication.

**Hurghada Museum.** Visitors interested in history and culture can explore a collection of antiquities reflecting aspects of ancient Egyptian civilisation. Check current hours and tickets with the museum before visiting.

### Sharm El Sheikh or Hurghada?

Your choice depends on the trip you want. Sharm El Sheikh may appeal to visitors interested in protected areas, diving, coral reefs and evenings out. Hurghada offers beaches, boat trips, islands and a range of activities for families and friends. Check current access and availability for each activity before booking.

### Before you travel

- Choose your hotel and area around the type of stay you want.
- Check availability for boat trips and activities, particularly in busy seasons.
- Prepare suitable sun protection for outdoor activities.
- If diving or snorkelling interests you, review operator arrangements and local rules.
- Leave time to explore local visitor areas and markets.

Both cities combine coastal scenery, marine activities and opportunities to relax. Choose the mix that best suits your group and schedule.
